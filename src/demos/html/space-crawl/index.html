The Seventh Age

Light of the Voyage

For three hundred years the star lanes have been silent. Since the last beacon on the outer rim went dark, merchant ships have drifted lost in the black, and one by one the colonies have fallen out of contact.

In the wreck of an abandoned freighter, a young navigator named Lin Zhou has found an ancient star chart. It marks a long-forgotten route that ends where, the legends say, the very first beacon was lit.

With the chart and a ship full of volunteers, she has set out into the silent deep. Behind her, one by one, the dark beacons are flickering back to life…
