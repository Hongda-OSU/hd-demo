Neon City never quite goes dark. When the sun sinks into the bay, its towers light up floor by floor, and the billboards along the old freight line flicker on in pink and cyan. By midnight the whole skyline hums like a single, enormous sign.

Down at street level, the city runs on hover traffic. Riderless bikes ferry parcels between districts on fixed routes, gliding a metre above the wet asphalt, while swarms of delivery drones weave through the gaps between rooftops overhead.
